description=[h1]🧩 Minidoracat MiniMap - MOD Compatibility for B42[/h1]
description=[h3]By Minidoracat[/h3]
description=
description=[hr][/hr]
description=
description=A third-party MOD compatibility pack for [b]Minidoracat MiniMap for B42[/b]: animals added by other MODs (such as dogs and horses) get their own name, icon, and filter toggle on the map.
description=
description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Required[/b]: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap for B42[/url]
description=[*] [b]Optional[/b]: the third-party MODs you want supported (see the list below); support for a MOD you don't have simply stays inactive
description=[/list]
description=Keep every Minidoracat MiniMap series mod up to date and restart the game after updating.
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Subscribe to and enable the main MOD, this pack, and the third-party MOD you want supported (in multiplayer, the server must enable all three)
description=[*] Turn on animal icons in the main MOD's animal icon settings
description=[*] “Dogs (loaded nearby)” and “Horses (loaded nearby)” appear in the species filter and can be toggled individually
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Key features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] Supports [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3740052292]Companion Dogs [ALPHA][/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3661336777]Horse Mod[/url]
description=[*] Dogs use the vanilla paw-print symbol or this pack's original colored dog icon; horses use this pack's original horse-head symbol or colored horse icon
description=[*] Does not replace third-party markers; Companion Dogs' own companion markers keep working
description=[*] Quietly does nothing when the matching third-party MOD is not installed — no extra options or errors
description=[*] Contains no third-party code, models, textures, or sounds
description=[/list]
description=📖 [b]Supported behavior, multiplayer notes, and how to request support:[/b] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3765182411/586187095760055711/]Compatibility Guide & Requests[/url]
description=

description=[list]
description=[*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]MiniMap for B42[/url] (main mod, required) — image-based world map and minimap, search, navigation
