description=A third-party MOD compatibility pack for the MiniMap: animals, NPCs, outposts and airdrops from other MODs show up on your map too.
description=
description=[h2]📦 Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Required[/b]: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3763913359]Minidoracat MiniMap[/url]
description=[*] [b]Optional[/b]: the third-party MODs you want supported (see the list below); support for a MOD you don't have simply stays inactive
description=[*] [b]Add/remove mid-save:[/b] safe either way
description=[*] [b]Languages:[/b] 繁體中文, 简体中文, English, 日本語, 한국어, Русский, Español, Português, Türkçe, Français, Polski, Deutsch (please report any translation issues)
description=[/list]
description=Keep every Minidoracat MiniMap series mod up to date and restart the game after updating.
description=
description=[h2]🚀 Quick start[/h2]
description=[olist]
description=[*] Subscribe to and enable the main MOD, this pack and the third-party MOD you want supported
description=[*] Turn on animal icons in the main MOD's animal icon settings
description=[*] “Dogs (loaded nearby)”, “Horses (loaded nearby)” and “Wolves (loaded nearby)” appear in the species filter and can be toggled individually
description=[*] To see NPC colors, turn on the main MOD's “Show zombie dots on the mini-map”
description=[/olist]
description=
description=[h2]✨ Key features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Supported MODs[/b]: [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3740052292]Companion Dogs[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3661336777]Horse Mod[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3268487204]Bandits[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3803984183]Project A-Life[/url], [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3791152383]Dynamic Outposts[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3789197845]Random Airdrops HF[/url]
description=[*] [b]NPC colors[/b]: NPCs from Bandits and Project A-Life get their own color among the zombie dots
description=[*] [b]Outposts and airdrops[/b]: outposts, infested areas and airdrop drop points also show on the mini-map
description=[*] [b]Dedicated icons[/b]: dogs use the vanilla paw print or a colored dog icon; horses use a horse-head symbol or a colored horse icon; wolves use a wolf-head symbol or a colored wolf icon
description=[*] [b]Markers left alone[/b]: third-party MODs' own markers keep working
description=[*] [b]Inactive when absent[/b]: without the matching MOD, no extra options and no errors
description=[*] [b]No third-party assets[/b]: contains no third-party code, models, textures or sounds
description=[/list]
